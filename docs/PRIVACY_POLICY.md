Effective Date: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Valentyn Chumachenko ("Licensor", "we",

VM-XCtrl, its licensing system, Optional Add-ons, website, purchase process,
and related services.

1. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

Depending on how VM-XCtrl is distributed and licensed, we may process:

* license keys and license status;
* information required to identify and manage a license or Optional Add-on
  entitlement;
* email address or other contact information provided during purchase or
  support requests;
* payment-related information provided through third-party payment providers;
* device or installation identifiers required for license activation;
* basic technical information required for activation, troubleshooting, or
  fraud prevention;
* information voluntarily provided when contacting us for support.

2. PAYMENT INFORMATION

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers.

We generally do not need to receive or store complete payment card details.

Payment providers may independently process payment and billing information
under their own privacy policies and terms.

3. LICENSE ACTIVATION

VM-XCtrl may communicate with licensing services to activate, validate, or
manage a license or Optional Add-on entitlement.

Technical information may be processed for purposes including:

* verifying that a license or Optional Add-on entitlement is valid;
* preventing unauthorized simultaneous use;
* detecting license abuse or fraud;
* allowing legitimate license transfers between devices;
* maintaining licensing infrastructure.

4. HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Information may be used to:

* provide and manage VM-XCtrl licenses and Optional Add-on entitlements;
* process purchases;
* activate and validate Software and Optional Add-ons;
* provide customer support;
* prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized distribution;
* maintain and improve the Software and related services;
* comply with legal obligations.

5. DATA RETENTION

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes
described in this Policy, including maintaining license records, accounting
records, security records, and legal compliance.

Some records may need to be retained for longer periods where required by
applicable law.

Those third parties may process information according to their own privacy
